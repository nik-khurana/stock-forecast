How the Graphs & Predictions Work

1. 3-Year Alpaca Data & Features

We pull 3 full years of daily bars (750+ sessions) via Alpaca's live market data API. We compute 21 stationary technical indicators: 14-day RSI, MACD lines, 20/50-day Moving Average ratios, Bollinger Bandwidth, ATR, and multi-period return lags.

2. 30-Day Quantile Forecasting

Rather than predicting arbitrary prices, models predict next-day log returns with quantile loss. The Mean is the expected median, while Min (10th percentile) and Max (90th percentile) form the confidence envelope expanding with $\sigma\sqrt{t}$.

3. 3-Year Monthly Diffusion Cones

For 3-year horizons, data is resampled into monthly closes. We fit an exponential trend drift $\ln(P_t) = \alpha + \beta t$ combined with geometric volatility diffusion cones, yielding monthly Min, Mean, and Max bounds for each of the next 36 months.
